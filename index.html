FAQ

What is a Binance Agent ID?

A Binance Agent ID is an identity NFT with a human-readable .ai name (issued via SPACE ID) that you can claim for free inside the Binance Wallet app. AgentDrop uses it as a sybil-resistant identity: one Agent ID, one claim.

Who can claim Drop #0?

Any wallet on BNB Chain that holds a Binance Agent ID. Each Agent ID can claim once — the rule is enforced by the smart contract, not by this website.

What do I receive, and what does it cost?

You receive 0.00001 BNB per claim, sent to your wallet when your claim transaction confirms. There is no claim fee — you only pay the normal BNB Chain network gas for the transaction.

When does Drop #0 end?

Drop #0 ends on 9 December 2026 at 15:00 WIB (08:00 UTC), or as soon as all 30 slots are claimed — whichever comes first.

Is the contract verified?

Yes. The AgentDrop contract source is verified on Sourcify (exact match) and readable on BscScan, so anyone can audit exactly what a claim does before signing. Links are in the footer below.
